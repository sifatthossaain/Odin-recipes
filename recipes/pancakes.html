Fluffy pancakes

Description

Light, soft, and delicious golden pancakes that make the ultimate breakfast treat. They take less than 15 minutes to prepare and pair perfectly with maple syrup, butter, or fresh berries.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • /2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups of milk

Tasks

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry mixture and stir until combined (do not overmix).
  3. Pour 1/4 cup of batter onto the skillet and cook until bubbles form on top.
  4. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown, then serve warm with syrup.
