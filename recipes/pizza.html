Pizza

Description

This classic homemade pizza features a golden, crispy crust topped with rich tomato sauce and melted mozzarella cheese. It is quick to prepare, fully customizable with your favorite toppings, and delivers an authentic pizzeria taste right from your own oven.

Ingredients

  • 1 portion of pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato pizza sauce
  • 1.5 cups mozzarella cheese (shredded)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning or dried oregano
  • Sliced pepperoni or your favorite vegetable toppings (bell peppers, mushrooms, onions)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Spread the tomato pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges for the crust.
  4. Bake in the oven for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust turns golden-brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
