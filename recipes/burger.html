Classic Beff Burger

Description

A juicy, homemade beef burger packed with flavor, served on a toasted bun with fresh lettuce, tomatoes, and melted cheese. It is simple to make and perfect for a quick, satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 200g ground beef
  • 1 burger bun
  • 1 cheese slice
  • tomato
  • letos

Steps

  1. Shape beef into a patty and season with salt/pepper.
  2. Cook patty on high heat for 3-4 minutes per side.
